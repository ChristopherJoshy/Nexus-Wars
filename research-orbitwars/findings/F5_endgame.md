# F5 — Endgame tactics, comeback & multi-player positioning

## Key claims

- **Desperado / trading-down (satirist):** when losing badly, throw everything
  at the strongest player to knock them down so YOU aren't the outright loser.
  Acceptable negative-EV trade when it flips the loser slot. Source:
  satirist.org strategy ("trading down").
- **Elimination bonus:** prioritize the *weakest remaining* player to force a
  head-to-head 1v1 where your skill edge dominates. Source: kvatsa5 README.
- **Leader-basher:** continually pressure the current leader to prevent
  snowball (don't let them accumulate surplus). Source: satirist.org.
- **Doom-evac (vkhydras reserve-walk):** when a planet is `doom` (will be
  captured next turn), immediately evac-launch remaining ships to the best
  escape target *before* losing them. Source: vkhydras #14.
- **Sniping (kvatsa5 sniper + finalize_chris coordinated_strikes):** two+
  fleets launched to arrive at the same turn on a high-value planet, flipping
  it clean and denying the owner the growth — higher value than a single
  larger fleet sent early (avoids the "overkill" / owner-recapture trap).
  Source: kvatsa5 README; finalize_chris.py.
- **Mega-hammer all-in:** commit ALL ships from a single strong planet in one
  committed push (no fraction head) to guarantee a capture. Source: AGENTS.md.
- **Accumulator:** bank surplus ships on a safe (home) planet and release them
  as a single wave once they exceed ~2× the cost to flip the next target —
  avoids "death by a thousand cuts." Source: kvatsa5 README.
- **Multi-prong:** two simultaneous threats force the opponent to split
  defense; whichever they don't defend loses the planet. Source: vkhydras #14.
